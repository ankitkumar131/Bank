Civic Heritage & Mission

Rooted in the Soil, Committed to Your Financial Autonomy

Parra Verlacanca Multipurpose Primary Agriculture Co-operative Society Ltd. is a community-focused co-operative institution committed to providing accessible financial services, savings solutions, lending facilities, and convenient payment services to individuals, employees, businesses, farmers, and families.

Guided by our foundational Sanskrit motto "संगच्छध्वं संवदध्वं" (March Together, Speak in Harmony), we bridge generations of traditional thrift with state-of-the-art technological convenience. We ensure that every Rupee deposited directly fuels local agriculture, family micro-enterprises, and civic upliftment.
