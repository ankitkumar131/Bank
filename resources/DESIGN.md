## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, community-rooted digital presence for a primary agricultural cooperative society. It bridges generational trust and civic pride with modern financial accessibility. The visual identity reflects security, heritage, stability, and mutual growth.

The design movement is **Corporate / Modern Institutional** fused with warm editorial prestige. It rejects cold, clinical fintech austerity in favor of warm civic credibility. Interfaces feature disciplined proportions, warm tactile backgrounds, regal serif headings, crisp utilitarian data structures, and focused vibrant warmth derived from the torch emblem and agricultural motifs.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from the institutional seal and emblem:

- **Primary (`#6b1d2f` / Deep Burgundy Maroon):** Represents institutional gravity, heritage, and capital security. Used for mastheads, primary action buttons, key metrics, and dominant brand framing.
- **Secondary (`#e26a2c` / Vibrant Torch Orange):** Evokes the eternal flame of cooperative solidarity and agrarian vitality. Used for primary callouts, pending status indicators, focus rings, and high-energy interactive elements.
- **Tertiary (`#f3aa27` / Warm Golden Yellow):** Symbolizes harvest, prosperity, and trust. Used for badges, notification accents, star indicators, and subtle decorative borders.
- **Neutral Dark (`#222224` / Dark Charcoal):** Used for primary typography, authoritative data readouts, and grounding dividers, preserving optimal contrast without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:** Crisp `#ffffff` for elevated interactive cards, balanced on subtle ivory foundations (`#faf8f5` and `#f4f1ea`) to convey tactile warmth and physical passbook reassurance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the heritage and institutional weight of **Merriweather** with the crisp clarity and numeric legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Merriweather):** Reserved for institutional titles, branch designations, interest yield highlights, and primary section anchors. Conveys legal certitude, tradition, and academic gravity.
- **Body & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Deployed across all transaction histories, forms, navigation headers, balance displays, and administrative data tables. Its tall x-height and open apertures ensure accessibility for agricultural members, field officers, and administrative clerks across varying screen qualities.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an institutional **12-column fluid grid** system bounded by a maximum container width of `1280px` on desktop displays.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Form views and banking records conform to 8-column or 6-column center-aligned administrative layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Double-column summaries collapse into vertical priority stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins. High-priority financial actions (deposit status, loan repayment, quick links) occupy full 4-column spans with sticky primary action bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **tonal layering**, **fine warm borders**, and **soft institutional ambient shadows** that mimic pristine stationery and security paper:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the foundation background (`#faf8f5`). Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Panels):** Pure white surfaces (`#ffffff`) resting on `#faf8f5`, bounded by a delicate 1px border (`rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.08)`) and supported by a warm diffuse shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(34, 34, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Panels):** Triggered when hovering over member loan schemes, deposit certificates, or active forms: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(34, 34, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Popovers):** Applied to transaction verification prompts, passbook exports, and critical member dialogues: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(34, 34, 36, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system is set to **Rounded (Level 2)**. This strikes a balance between professional banking rigor and welcoming cooperative warmth.

- Base inputs, buttons, and utility chips adopt `0.5rem` (8px).
- Main content containers, deposit cards, and analytical modules utilize `1rem` (`rounded-lg`, 16px) or `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`, 24px) for expansive hero cards.
- Status indicator tags and currency pill indicators employ full organic pill shapes (`9999px`) to maintain contrast against formal linear financial tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Burgundy (`#6b1d2f`) fill, white text, 8px radius. Hover shifts to darkened burgundy (`#541423`) with a subtle 2px accent shadow.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Torch Orange (`#e26a2c`) fill, white text, used strictly for conversion actions like "Apply for Crop Loan" or "Deposit Now."
- **Outline / Institutional:** 1.5px solid border in Primary (`#6b1d2f`) with transparent background, turning to faint burgundy tint (`rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.04)`) on hover.
- **Ghost:** Charcoal text with zero border, displaying an underline or subtle background fill upon interaction.

### Cards & Passbook Modules
- Built with `rounded-xl` (16px to 24px), crisp white surface, and a 1px border in `rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.12)`.
- Key account cards feature a top 4px accent trim in Golden Yellow (`#f3aa27`) or Deep Burgundy (`#6b1d2f`) to distinguish account types (e.g., Agricultural Credit vs. Recurring Deposit).

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Height of 44px (desktop) / 48px (mobile) for touch compliance.
- Warm gray background fill (`#faf8f5`) with a 1px border (`#e2ded6`).
- Focused states produce an active Torch Orange border (`#e26a2c`) accompanied by an orange glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(226, 106, 44, 0.18)`).

### Badges & Chips
- Status indicators feature muted background washes paired with saturated typography:
  - Active / Cleared: Warm Olive / Gold tint (`bg: #fef8eb`, `text: #9a6509`, `border: #f3aa27`).
  - Pending: Warm Orange tint (`bg: #fdf2eb`, `text: #b64711`).
  - Institutional Notice: Burgundy tint (`bg: #f7ebed`, `text: #6b1d2f`).

### Tables & Ledgers
- Minimalist ledger rows with alternate ivory-white striping. 
- Headers use bold small-caps in Dark Charcoal with a 2px bottom border in Primary Burgundy.
- Numerical figures are right-aligned using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px hit target with 4px border radius for checkboxes and 50% circular bounds for radios.
- Inactive state: 1.5px border in charcoal mute (`#a49f99`).
- Checked state: Solid `#6b1d2f` fill with crisp white internal checks, ensuring unmistakable status clarity for loan terms and audit verifications.
